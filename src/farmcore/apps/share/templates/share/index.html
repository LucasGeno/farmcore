Sharing

You choose who may read your farm data, for what, and until when. Machine and station data go to the third party you choose (Data Act Art. 5); revoke at any time. Every read is logged below.

What

Only read access is available. Nothing here allows someone to change your records.

  • Parcels

    Unavailable · No sharing preset for parcels.

  • Sprays / register

    Share confirmed spray logs and the register, read only.

    Spray register CSV (Reg. 2023/564) Export NGSI-LD

  • Weather

    Share weather station data, read only.

  • KPIs

    Unavailable · No sharing preset for KPIs.

With whom

People and services with read access. Permissions use real-world validity dates.
