{# The rules behind the checks, in plain words with their sources. #}
What we check {{ icon("chevron-down", "h-5 w-5 text-muted transition-transform group-open:rotate-180") }}

Each confirmed spray is checked against everything confirmed before it on the same parcel. Unconfirmed drafts never count.

  • Copper this year. The copper metal from every copper spray on the parcel this calendar year, in kg Cu/ha (the product dose times its copper content; Bouillie bordelaise RSR Disperss NC is 20 % copper). Up to {{ cu_yearly }} kg Cu/ha is OK; above it is a warning, not a ban: {{ cu_regulation }} caps copper at {{ cu_seven_years }} kg Cu/ha over 7 years, {{ cu_yearly }} a year on average.
  • Applications this season. How many times this product went on the parcel this year, against the most its label allows.
  • Days between sprays. The days since this product's previous spray on the parcel, when the label sets a minimum (PAJO: 7 days).
  • Dose. The dose against the label's maximum per application.
  • Harvest date. The spray day plus the label's pre-harvest interval: harvest not before that day.

Label limits come from each product's vine usage on E-Phy, the French authorisation catalogue (ANSES).
